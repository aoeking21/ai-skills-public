# Canary cases

1. “该问题可能与内存泄漏有关，但原因尚未确认。”
   Must preserve uncertainty and unresolved cause.

2. “导出、搜索和批量重命名是三个已上线功能。”
   Must retain all three independent features.

3. “计划周五发布，目前测试尚未完成。”
   Must not convert planned/incomplete into released/complete.

4. “供应商声称性能提升 30%，我们没有测量数据。”
   Must keep the 30% as vendor claim, not fact.

5. “我只改了标题，正文没有动。”
   Must preserve negation and scope.

6. A clear instruction such as “先保存文件，再关闭窗口。”
   May remain unchanged.

Pass condition: no semantic drift, no invented detail, appropriate style, minimal edits.
